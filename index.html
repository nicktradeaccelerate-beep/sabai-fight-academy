Hua Hin recovery & training FAQs

Good to know.

Is there a Finnish sauna in Hua Hin at Sabai?

Yes. Sabai Retreat has a Finnish sauna on site in Bo Fai, Hua Hin.

Does Sabai have a cold plunge / ice bath?

Yes. The cold plunge can be used as part of a recovery visit or combined with Muay Thai training. Message Sabai for current access and pricing.

Can I book Muay Thai plus sauna and cold plunge?

Yes. Ask for the Train + Recover option and Sabai can combine your training session with sauna and cold plunge.

Can Sabai host retreats or private events?

Yes. Sabai has training space, recovery facilities, lake access and a cooking / meal area suitable for private groups, visiting coaches, wellness days and small events.

Does Sabai offer training for young fighters?

Enquire with Sabai about age-appropriate junior training and availability. Parent or guardian involvement and appropriate coaching / supervision should be part of the programme.

Can Sabai run fight exhibitions or showcase events?

Sabai can use controlled demonstrations, pad work, technique showcases and visiting-fighter events. Any competitive activity should follow appropriate local rules, consent and supervision.
