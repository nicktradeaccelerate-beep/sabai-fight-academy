## Search / measurement baseline
- Connected GSC Wizard / Search Console dashboard currently contains zero properties.
- No GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics or Google Search Console verification tag is present in the site source.
- No first-party clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexed-page history or conversion attribution is therefore available yet.

## Technical SEO
### Already good
- Production domain resolves over HTTPS.
- robots.txt returns 200 and allows crawling.
- sitemap.xml returns 200 and contains the site's indexable pages.
- Canonical URLs point to retreat.raifinder.com.
- Homepage has a strong search-intent title and meta description around Muay Thai, sauna, cold plunge and Hua Hin.
- Dedicated landing pages exist for:
  - Muay Thai Hua Hin
  - Finnish sauna & cold plunge Hua Hin
  - Kids / junior Muay Thai Hua Hin
  - Muay Thai retreats Hua Hin
  - Events / group experiences Hua Hin
- Each landing page has its own title, description, H1 and canonical.
- Structured data uses schema.org SportsActivityLocation and includes name, phone, email, address and page URL.
- Homepage schema includes price range, Instagram sameAs and amenity features.
- Internal links connect the service pages.
- Site contains English/Thai presentation on the main page.
- Real photographs, pricing, timetable, FAQs, directions and direct contact routes are present.

### Gaps / next opportunities
- No Search Console property connected.
- No GA4 / GTM measurement.
- No conversion events for WhatsApp, LINE, call, email or retreat enquiries.
- LINE button is generic and is not yet tied to Tony's actual LINE profile / ID.
- Site does not yet show the existing review strength prominently as social proof.
- Open Graph implementation is incomplete: homepage and subpages lack a consistent og:url / og:image strategy; Twitter metadata is also limited.
- Structured data can be strengthened with geo coordinates, opening hours, map URL, offers / service catalogue and richer sameAs references once verified.
- FAQ content exists but is not currently marked up as FAQPage structured data.
- Search visibility is at launch baseline; exact-domain search did not yet surface the new site.
- A dedicated brand domain may eventually be worth evaluating, although retreat.raifinder.com is technically valid and live.
- No first-party booking form / CRM conversion trail currently ties an enquiry to revenue.
- No explicit attribution between Google Business Profile, organic search, Instagram and bookings.

## Current positioning strengths
- Distinctive combined proposition: Muay Thai + recovery rather than gym-only.
- Searchable local services: Muay Thai, sauna, cold plunge, retreats, kids training and events.
- Existing external review signal is strong.
- Pricing and timetable reduce friction.
- Direct owner contact keeps the funnel simple.
- Retreat / group / wellness angle increases average order value beyond drop-in classes.

## Baseline conclusion
Sabai starts from a stronger SEO foundation than Bart's site: it already has canonical URLs, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, multiple intent-led landing pages and structured data. The largest missing layer is measurement and Google integration rather than page creation.

The immediate objective is to connect Search Console + GA4/GTM, verify the Google Business Profile / website relationship, add review proof and conversion tracking, then measure whether the new site earns visibility for high-intent Hua Hin searches and turns that traffic into confirmed bookings.

This gives us a clean before/after case study for a different vertical from Bart:
- Bart = mobile local service
- Sabai = destination / fitness / wellness / experience business
